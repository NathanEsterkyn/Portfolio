Arduino · 3.3V Systems · Waterproof Design

Designed and implemented a low-power ultrasonic water level sensor capable of measuring depths from 0.5–5 meters. The system interfaces via serial output and operates on a 3.3V supply for battery-powered applications.

Key challenges included noise rejection, beam angle optimization, and long-term environmental reliability.
